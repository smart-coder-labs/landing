## Product Purpose

SmartCoderLabs is a software product factory. The supplied Spanish copy describes bringing design, engineering and applied AI together to turn concrete problems into useful tools, with attention to the people who operate, maintain and evolve the result.

- Backend, APIs and data: system connections, contracts, integrations and explainable data decisions.
- Applied AI: context, memory and tools, with evaluation, explicit limits and human review according to the product's needs.
- Team enablement: build, operate, evolve and share the knowledge needed to continue.
